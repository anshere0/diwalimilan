## 🚀 Key Streamlined Features

1. **Compact, High-Energy Design:**

   - High-energy youth aesthetic tailored for guys aged 20–21.

   - Everyone walks the carpet upon arrival!

5. **Games & Mystery Dare Vault:**
   - Teases party games: *Never Have I Ever*, *Hook Step Face-Off*, *Silent Dumb Charades*, *Mafia*.
   - Interactive Game Suggestion form with live community upvoting.

   - Only shows names of brothers who actually confirm their RSVP!
   - Real-time `[X GENTLEMEN CONFIRMED]` counter.

   - Clear rule highlighted in Hero, Etiquette section, and RSVP pledge:
     - Doors open at 6:00 PM sharp (don't be late!).
     - Every brother helps clean up the venue at 11:00 PM.
     - Respect hospital & old-age home next door (zero loud firecrackers, LEDs/diyas only).

8. **Executive Host & Admin Deck (Passcode: `diwali2026`):**
   - Click the shield icon in the header.
   - 1-click switch to **Turn ON / OFF Live Voting for Awards**.
   - 1-click switch to **Turn ON / OFF Full Dare Vault**.

   - Export Supplies Checklist to CSV.
